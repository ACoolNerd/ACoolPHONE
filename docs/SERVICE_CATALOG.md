# ACoolPHONE Service Catalog

## Commercial status

These are working launch offers for scoping and sales communication. A signed proposal, statement of work, data/security terms, and payment schedule control the engagement. Posted prices do not create a binding offer.

## Blueprint Sprint — $2,500 fixed fee

**Target duration:** 10 business days after kickoff and receipt of required access/context.

**Included**

- stakeholder and workflow discovery;
- rights/authority and integration dependency matrix;
- current-state and target data-flow map;
- security, privacy, compliance, and provider-term issue-spotting;
- recommended tenant, adapter, queue, worker, evidence, and deployment design;
- phased implementation backlog, estimate, risks, and acceptance plan;
- one review session and one consolidated revision.

**Acceptance evidence**

- blueprint document;
- architecture diagram;
- decision/risk register;
- implementation and acceptance backlog.

**Not included**

- production implementation;
- provider, legal, compliance, or security certification;
- paid external research, licenses, or provider fees;
- data migration or remediation.

## Pilot Build — starting at $9,500

**Typical duration:** 4–6 weeks after blueprint acceptance, access, and provider readiness.

**Baseline included**

- Blueprint Sprint deliverables;
- one tenant workspace and operator preview adapted to the client;
- one approved provider integration;
- one event-to-outcome workflow with defined human handoff;
- scoped database records and migration;
- staging and production deployment for the accepted topology;
- acceptance tests, operator training, launch checklist, and runbook;
- 30-day defect warranty for accepted scope.

**Change triggers**

- more providers, tenants, workflows, roles, environments, or data sources;
- historical migration or data cleanup;
- custom identity/SSO, residency, high availability, or compliance evidence;
- hardware procurement, field setup, or device logistics;
- material provider approval delays or changed requirements.

## Managed Operations — from $1,500/month + $3,500 onboarding

**Baseline included**

- platform and workflow monitoring during contracted coverage hours;
- incident triage and escalation coordination;
- provider-usage and operational metric reconciliation;
- monthly operating review;
- up to eight hours monthly for accepted minor improvements;
- quarterly control and roadmap review.

Unused improvement hours do not roll over unless the agreement says otherwise. Project work, new adapters, major migrations, after-hours response, and provider fees are separate.

## Cost disclosure

Unless a signed proposal expressly includes them, the client pays directly or reimburses:

- devices, SIMs, carrier plans, numbers, and accessories;
- cloud hosting, databases, queues, logs, storage, backups, and bandwidth;
- WhatsApp, Twilio, Stripe, OpenAI, Google, GitHub, or other provider charges;
- taxes, chargebacks, refunds, message-template fees, and verification fees;
- third-party legal, privacy, compliance, penetration-test, or certification work;
- travel, shipping, installation, and on-site support.

## Client responsibilities

- designate an authorized owner and responsive product/operator lead;
- provide lawful access to owned accounts, systems, and controlled devices;
- disclose relevant policies, contracts, consent records, provider limits, and data risks;
- complete provider verification and approvals;
- review decisions and acceptance evidence on schedule;
- approve messaging content, pricing, workflow rules, and human escalation;
- maintain required business, legal, privacy, security, and insurance obligations.

## Change control

Every material change states:

1. requested outcome;
2. reason and authority;
3. scope and acceptance impact;
4. schedule impact;
5. one-time and recurring cost impact;
6. security/privacy/provider impact;
7. rollback or migration impact;
8. written approval.
